## Musicians and Cultural Movement

### Pessoal do Ceará Movement
- **Wikipedia - Pessoal do Ceará**: https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pessoal_do_Ceará
  - Group of young artists from Ceará who emerged in early 1970s
  - One of the most important movements in contemporary Brazilian music
  - Members: Amelinha, Belchior, Fagner, Fausto Nilo, Ednardo, Rodger Rogério, and Téti
  - Influenced by philosopher Augusto Pontes

### Fagner
- **Boomerang Music**: https://boomerangmusic.com.br/fagner-lanca-album-com-relicario-de-cancoes-e-historias-que-o-conectam-ao-parceiro-belchior/
  - Released "Além desse futuro" album (2024), first solo album in 10 years
  - Famous collaboration "Mucuripe" with Belchior (recorded by Elis Regina)

- **Metropoles**: https://www.metropoles.com/metropoles-music/fagner-no-estilo-brasil-do-ceara-as-cancoes-ineditas-com-belchior
  - Performed at Festival Estilo Brasil (October 24, 2024)

### Belchior
- **Itaú Cultural Encyclopedia**: https://enciclopedia.itaucultural.org.br/pessoas/8531-belchior
  - Major figure in Brazilian music
  - Part of Pessoal do Ceará movement
  - His works have been performed by artists like Elis Regina and Vanusa

## Slang and Expressions

### Oxente / Ôxe
- **Portal F5**: https://portalf5.com.br/dicionario-do-dialeto-cearense/
- **DPopular**: https://www.dicionariopopular.com/dicionario-cearense-girias/
  - Expression of surprise or astonishment
  - Can signify doubt, excitement, or indignation depending on intonation
  - Popular throughout the entire Northeast

### Vixe Maria
- **Multiple sources**
  - Means "Virgem Maria" (Virgin Mary)
  - Expression of astonishment
  - "Vixe" comes from "virgem" (virgin)

### Arretado
- **Ceará Rent a Car**: https://ceararentacar.com.br/sem-categoria/girias-cearenses/
- **ABIH-CE**: https://www.abih-ce.com.br/dicionario-ceares
- **Viajando com a Mala Rosa**: https://viajandocomamalarosa.com.br/girias-cearenses/
  - Quintessential Ceará expression
  - Greatest compliment that can be given to a person
  - Means "the best", beautiful, intelligent, and praiseworthy
  - Applies to both people and things

## Traditional Food

### General Ceará Cuisine
- **Wikipedia - Culinária do Ceará**: https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culin%C3%A1ria_do_Cear%C3%A1
  - Diversified culinary tradition shaped by geographic location
  - Fusion of indigenous, African, and Portuguese roots

### Baião de Dois
- **Multiple sources**: https://www.doiz.com.br/blog-posts/culinaria-tipica-do-ceara-pratos-e-bebidas-que-voce-precisa-provar
  - Icon of northeastern cuisine
  - Made with rice and green beans (feijão verde)
  - Enriched with cream or butter for creamy texture
  - Often served with carne de sol and queijo coalho

### Tapioca
- **Vida de Viajante**: https://vidadeviajante.com.br/comidas-tipicas-do-ceara/
- **360meridianos**: https://www.360meridianos.com/especial/comidas-tipicas-do-ceara
  - Made with hydrated cassava gum
  - Can be filled with sweet or savory options
  - Common at breakfast or as snack
  - One of Ceará's gastronomic symbols

### Carne de Sol
- **Fortaleza Dicas**: https://fortalezadicas.com.br/comidas-tipicas-do-ceara-2/
  - Meat cured and dried in the sun
  - Important ingredient in semi-arid region of Brazil
  - Symbol of sertão and caatinga cuisine
  - Used in many traditional dishes

### Cajuína
- **Multiple sources**
  - Made from clarified and pasteurized cashew juice
  - No alcohol
  - Known for sweet and smooth flavor
  - Strong cultural symbolism
  - Considered intangible cultural heritage of Brazil

### Other Traditional Foods

## Tourist Attractions and Places

### Jericoacoara
- **Bahia.ws**: https://bahia.ws/en/category/northeast-brazil/ceara/
- **Visit Brasil**: https://visitbrasil.com/en/DestinosLP/fortaleza-praia-e-sol-ingles/
  - Located ~490km from Fortaleza
  - Famous for crystal-clear lagoons
  - Sunset Dune, Pedra Furada (Arched Rock), Lagoa do Paraíso
  - Popular for kitesurfing and windsurfing (especially July)
  - Pedra Furada is the symbol of Jericoacoara
  - Rock shelters used for thousands of years by human populations

### Canoa Quebrada
- **TripAdvisor**: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g635974-d552354-Reviews-Praia_de_Canoa_Quebrada-Canoa_Quebrada_State_of_Ceara.html
- **Do in Brazil**: https://doinbrazil.com/fortaleza-brazil/
  - Former hippie stronghold
  - ~165km (103 miles) east of Fortaleza
  - Famous for red cliffs, wide sandy beaches, relaxed vibe
  - Popular for kite surfing and horseback riding
  - Lively street called Broadway with bars and shops
  - Symbolized by famous half moon and star

### Praia do Futuro
- **Multiple sources**
  - 15 minutes from downtown Fortaleza
  - Most popular beach for swimming in Fortaleza
  - Known for large "barracas" (beach clubs)
  - Offers lounge chairs, pools, and fresh seafood
  - Hotspot for both locals and tourists
